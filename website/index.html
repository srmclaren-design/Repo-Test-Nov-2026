Four chapters.
One planet below.

From the launch pad to splashdown, every minute is choreographed by people who have flown it. You only have to look out of the window.

in orbit.

A 360° window onto the world

The largest window ever flown: 4.2 metres of fused silica, ringed by soft handholds so you can float in front of the view for as long as you like.

Private Suites

Soundproofed, softly lit, and with a sleeping pod that holds you like a hammock.

Orbital dining

A tasting menu designed by a two-star chef and re-engineered for weightlessness.

Two veteran astronauts on every voyage, plus a lifeboat capsule docked the entire time.
